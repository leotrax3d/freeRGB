## Audit result

qualifications; `identify()` claims no device. The family spans controllers with
different packet sizes and transports (59/65, 154, 520, 1032 bytes), differing color
plane orders and init/profil packets; the audited branch version had duplicated rows
(26) and reused builders across controllers.
