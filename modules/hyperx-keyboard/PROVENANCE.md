## Supported subset

`supported = true` only for Alloy Elite RGB (0x0951:0x16BE, interface 2, page 0xFF01)
and Alloy FPS RGB (0x0951:0x16DC, interface 2, page 0xFF01) — 2 detector rows.

## Nicht unterstützt (audit-Gründe)

* Alloy Origins / Origins HP (0x16E5/0x0591): upstream inserts 19 blank gaps into a
  126-color buffer, sends exactly 9 packets with zero padding, zone is 107 LEDs;
  branch sent unpadded chunks and 106 LEDs.
* Alloy Origins 60/65 (0x1734/0x0C8E/0x038F): upstream zones are 71/77 LEDs and
  always 5 packets; branch had 61/67 and 4 packets.
* Alloy Elite 2 (0x1711/0x058F): branch init packet was the Origins2/Eve init
  (`44 01 04`); upstream init is `00 04 F2` (feature reports).
* Origins 2 65 HP (0x0CC2): upstream sends init `44 01 04` before colors, exactly 4
  packets and 74 LEDs (branch: 106).
* Eve 1800 HP (0x08C2): missing init packet `44 01 04`.
* Alloy Origins Core (0x16E6/0x098F): upstream 380-byte buffer with segment/sector
  layout (pos = (v/12)*48 + ((v/6)&1)*8, G at pos, R at pos+16, B at pos+32) and
  7 constant packets; the branch flat layout is wrong.

## Changes

* Direct mode only; keepalive threads (50 ms re-sends) and 10 ms sleeps between
  channel packets are not modeled by the frame-pushed module API.
* Audit corrections over the first branch version: duplicated detector rows removed
  (20 faithful rows), supported set restricted, upstream zone/matrix layouts ported,
  feature-report transport used for the supported devices, fabricated init removed,
  fallthrough frame arm removed.
