## Changes

* Direct mode only; effects profiles and hardware-save flows omitted.
* Removed the fabricated generic 64-byte report-0x02 fallthrough (`report::direct`);
  uncovered frame paths now fail closed with a protocol error.
* No serial number reads; HID++ responses matched on report-id/feature only.
